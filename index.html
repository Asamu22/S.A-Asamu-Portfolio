About

A Career Built on
Getting It There.

For over 25 years, Samuel Akinbode Asamu has been one of Lagos's most dependable dispatch professionals — a rider whose name has moved sensitive documents through the corridors of Nigeria's biggest cooperative societies and whose wheels have kept businesses, institutions, and everyday people moving.

From delivering critical documents for the Lagos State Cooperative Federation and the Cooperative Federation of Nigeria, to time-sensitive food logistics and personal errands for individual clients, Samuel brings the same standard to every job: punctual, discreet, professional.

Formally trained and certified through the Lascofed Training & Development Centre, he is not merely experienced — he is credentialled. Now available as a freelance dispatch rider for anyone in Lagos who needs a professional they can trust.
